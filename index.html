Workshop Overview

The ocean needs robots that can do more than navigate. Mission Ocean 2030 explores how perception, autonomy, physical interaction and scientific understanding can come together in reliable, field-deployable systems.

From fragile ecosystems to deep-sea exploration, the workshop brings together robotics and marine science communities to identify the capabilities, benchmarks and collaborations needed by 2030.

01 · Ocean science & exploration

Robotic systems designed around real scientific missions: ecological monitoring, habitat mapping, sampling, archaeology and persistent observation.

02 · Perception, mapping & autonomy

Robust vision, sonar, SLAM, multisensor fusion and adaptive mission planning in turbid, dynamic, GPS-denied environments.

03 · Sampling & sustainable intervention

Dexterous manipulation, contact-aware autonomy, low-disturbance sampling, shared control and safe interaction with fragile marine targets.

04 · Simulation & field deployment

Digital twins, physical AI, benchmarks, synthetic data and reproducible pathways from laboratory prototypes to ocean missions.
